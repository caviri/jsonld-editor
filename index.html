JSON-LD Explorer

Design ontologies by reuse · edit and validate linked data

  • 📂
    Open anything — JSON-LD, Turtle, N-Triples, N-Quads, TriG or a .rete file (menu, drag & drop, or ?src=URL).
  • 🔎
    Find terms — Ctrl+K searches this document, 15 bundled vocabularies, any vocabulary you load, OLS and Wikidata.
  • ＋
    Create — classes, properties and instances; similar existing terms are suggested so you reuse before you mint.
  • ⌨️
    Autocomplete — Ctrl+Space in the editor; missing prefixes are added for you.
  • 🧬
    Schema view — the class model: subclasses, domain → range, links observed in the data, and SHACL shapes on their target classes.
  • ▭
    Box diagram — nodes as cards with their values, joined by right-angled links (a UML-style class diagram in the schema view). Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, drag cards to move them.
  • 🐢
    Turtle view — switch the source editor between JSON-LD and Turtle; drag its edge to resize, or ⇔ for half screen.
  • ⚠
    Problems — invalid IRIs, keys that vanish in RDF, undefined prefixes, misspelled vocabulary terms, with one-click fixes.
  • ⬡
    rete — SPARQL and SHACL in the browser, export to .rete (with a Dataset Card), Turtle or N-Triples. github.com/caviri/rete
  • ↩
    Undo — every edit from the workbench is undoable with Ctrl+Z in the editor; your work is autosaved in this browser.
